# mia.cx

Mia Riezebos's personal site: work, writing, biography and contact, plus what each page prints.

## Printing

**Article**:
A page with a body to read, such as a work write-up, a blog post or a Textile policy. It prints itself; every other page prints the CV.
_Avoid_: Post, content page

**CV**:
Mia's curriculum vitae, printed by every page that is not an Article.
_Avoid_: Résumé, profile

**Appendix**:
Pages printed after the CV on an index page, listing that index's items with their taglines.
_Avoid_: Addendum, annex, listing
