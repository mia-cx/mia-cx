# mia.cx

Mia Riezebos's personal site: work, writing, biography and contact, plus what each page prints.

## Content

**Project**:
A piece of Mia's work in the vault's `work/` folder: a flat note, or a folder whose `index.md` is its overview. /work lists projects.
_Avoid_: Work note, portfolio item

**Project post**:
A post written in a project's folder. It lives at `/work/<project>/<post>` and is listed on its project's page and on /blog.
_Avoid_: Work post, devlog

## Printing

**Article**:
A page with a body to read, such as a work write-up, a blog post or a Textile policy. It prints itself; every other page prints the CV.
_Avoid_: Post, content page

**CV**:
Mia's curriculum vitae, printed by every page that is not an Article.
_Avoid_: Résumé, profile

**Theme**:
How the CV is rendered: **Colour** puts the field on dark paper, **Print** multiplies it into white paper like ink. Cmd+P always prints the Print theme.
_Avoid_: Mode, version, light/dark

**Appendix**:
Pages printed after the CV on an index page, listing that index's items with their taglines.
_Avoid_: Addendum, annex, listing
