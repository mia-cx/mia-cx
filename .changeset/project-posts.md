A project can now have posts. Each one has a page under its project, `/work/<project>/<post>`, which prints like any other post and links back to the project. The project's page lists its posts under the write-up, newest first, and /blog, its printed appendix and /llms.txt list them beside the other posts. Wikilinks now also resolve between work and blog notes.
