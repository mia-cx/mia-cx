Vesta helps independent musicians, small labels and creative publishers manage their catalog, build their brand and reach their audience from one place.

It starts with the two problems that hurt most: conversion, through smart links that remove the friction of finding a release on the right platform, and identity, through pages a creator can actually make their own. Pre-saves, collections, feeds, and eventually streaming and commerce follow from there.

Vesta is a collective venture aiming to make knowledge, experience and skill accessible for young artists.

Indie creators keep profiles on ten platforms, write metadata into spreadsheets, and pay for features they never use. That fragmentation is the problem worth solving.

Every existing platform looks the same. A creator should be able to change colour, font, layout and theme, because the page should feel like their home.
